## 🛡️ Armor — Maximum Armor Level (AL)

> Armor Level is added to your base weenie AL at item creation. All regular armor uses the same mutation table — T6 rolls a bonus of +100 to +160 in ten-point steps, then a second pass always adds a further **±5**, giving a total range of **+95 to +165 AL**. Covenant armor works differently — it sets AL absolutely rather than adding to a base.

> **⭐ Covenant Armor (Special):** Instead of adding to base AL, covenant armor sets AL directly, and the AL band and wield requirement are rolled together. At T6 the bands run 100 / 200 / 300 / 350 / 400 / 440 / 470, each with a small random bonus on top (+0–100 on the low bands, +0–30 on the top two). The **best possible roll is AL 500** — the 470 band plus a +30 bonus — but it is only a 10% chance at T6, so most covenant drops land far lower.
>
> Each band comes in three variants by defense skill, and the wield requirement depends on which one you get:
>
> | AL Band | Melee Def wield | Missile Def wield | Magic Def wield |
> |---------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
> | 470 (→ 470–500) | 400 | 330 | 300 |
> | 440 (→ 440–470) | 370 | 305 | 280 |
> | 400 (→ 400–440) | 350 | 290 | 265 |
> | 350 (→ 350–400) | 325 | 270 | 245 |
>
> Covenant armor also randomly gains physical resist bonuses (Slash/Pierce/Bludgeon +0.1 to +0.3, 40% chance each) and has a small chance for elemental resistance up to a +1.0 mod (1% each for Fire/Cold/Acid/Electric). Its burden is also reduced to roughly 35–65% of base.

## 🎯 Weapon Combat Modifiers

> Every weapon can roll additional combat modifiers on top of damage. These apply to all melee weapon types (swords, axes, maces, spears, daggers, staves, unarmed) with identical max values.

### Attack Modifier (WeaponOffense)

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | +0 to +0.05 |
| T2 | +0.02 to +0.07 |
| T3 | +0.03 to +0.10 |
| T4 | +0.05 to +0.12 |
| T5 | +0.06 to +0.15 |
| **T6 Max** | **+0.15 → final 1.15** |

Base is 1.0. A 1.15 attack mod means 15% better chance to land hits.

### Melee Defense Modifier (WeaponDefense)

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | +0 to +0.05 |
| T2 | +0.02 to +0.07 |
| T3 | +0.03 to +0.10 |
| T4 | +0.05 to +0.12 |
| T5 | +0.06 to +0.15 |
| **T6 Max** | **+0.15 → final 1.15** |

Same range as attack mod. Stacks with your base melee defense skill.

### Weapon Speed Modifier

> **⭐ All T6 weapons are guaranteed to get a speed modifier (100% chance).** Lower is faster. The quality roll at T6 goes up to level 9, with the formula:
>
> ```
> speedMod = 1.0 − (qualityLevel × 0.025 ± 0.025 random)
> ```
>
> At quality 9 (max T6 quality): speed mod = **0.75 to 0.80** (20–25% faster than unmodded base).

| Tier | Chance of Getting Speed Bonus | Max Quality | Best Speed Mod at That Tier |
|------|-------------------------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| T1 | 50% | 1 | 0.95 |
| T2 | 60% | 2 | 0.925 |
| T3 | 70% | 4 | 0.875 |
| T4 | 80% | 6 | 0.825 |
| T5 | 90% | 7 | 0.80 |
| **T6** | **100%** | **9** | **0.75 (best in game)** |

### Missile Defense & Magic Defense (10% chance)

> All melee weapons have a **10% chance** to also roll a Missile Defense Modifier and/or Magic Defense Modifier, both in the range **1.005–1.025**. These are small bonuses but apply at all tiers equally.
>
> ⚠️ **Melee only.** Bows, crossbows, atlatls and wands cannot roll these on ClassicPvP — see the note at the end of the missile section.

## 🪄 Wands & Casters

> Wands can roll up to three types of bonuses: Elemental Damage Mod (only on elemental wands), Mana Conversion Mod, and Melee Defense Mod. All three roll independently.

### Elemental Damage Modifier — *elemental wands only*

> ⚠️ The elemental damage mod MULTIPLIES your spell damage for the matching element. A 1.10 mod means 10% more damage. Higher mods require higher wield requirements.

**T6 breakdown**

| Mod | Wield Required | Chance |
|-----|----------------|--------|
| 1.01–1.02 | None | 13% |
| 1.03–1.05 | 310 skill | 50% |
| 1.06–1.08 | 330 skill | 31% |
| **1.09–1.10** | **355 skill** | **6%** (1.10 alone is 2%) |

At lower tiers: T1–T4 cap at 1.02 with no wield requirement, and T5 adds the 1.03–1.05 / 310-skill band. 1.10 is the absolute maximum in the game and only appears on a 355-wield wand.

### Mana Conversion Modifier

> Reduces mana cost of spells. Rolls independently on all wands (elemental or not).

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | 0–0.02 |
| T2 | 0–0.04 |
| T3 | 0–0.06 |
| T4 | 0.02–0.08 |
| T5 | 0.04–0.10 |
| **T6 Max** | **0.05–0.10** |

*Max Mana Conversion bonus at T6: +0.10. Stacks with your Mana Conversion Mastery skill.*

### Melee Defense Modifier

> Wands also grant melee defense. Unlike melee weapons, the wand mutation **sets** the value outright rather than adding to a base, but the cap is the same.

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | 1.00–1.05 |
| T2 | 1.01–1.06 |
| T3 | 1.03–1.10 |
| T4 | 1.05–1.12 |
| T5 | 1.07–1.14 |
| **T6 Max** | **1.08–1.15** |

### Missile & Magic Defense

> ⚠️ Wands **never** roll Missile Defense or Magic Defense modifiers on ClassicPvP — same hardcoded-script quirk described in the missile weapon section. Only melee weapons get them.

## 🏹 Missile Weapons — Bows, Crossbows & Atlatls

> Missile weapons roll a **Damage Modifier** (multiplier to arrow/bolt/dart damage) and, for elemental variants, an **Elemental Damage Bonus** (flat bonus to the element). Elemental and non-elemental weapons use different mutation tables, and **each weapon family has its own Damage Modifier range** — a crossbow is not a bow with a different icon.
>
> ⚠️ **At Tier 6 every missile weapon that drops is elemental.** The non-elemental tables below stop being reachable after T5, so the elemental tables are the only ones that matter for T6 loot.

### Elemental — Damage Modifier

> This roll is the same at every tier; it does **not** improve with tier, and it carries no wield requirement of its own.

| Weapon Family | Damage Modifier Range | Best Roll |
|---------------|----------------------|-----------|
| Crossbow | 2.47 / 2.50 / 2.53 / 2.55 | **2.55** |
| Atlatl | 2.43 / 2.45 / 2.47 / 2.50 | **2.50** |
| Bow | 2.23 / 2.25 / 2.27 / 2.30 | **2.30** |

*The two middle values are 35% each; the top and bottom are 15% each.*

### Elemental — Damage Bonus (T6)

> The Elemental Damage Bonus is what sets the wield requirement. All elemental missile weenies ship with a **base wield of 315**, so even the lowest T6 bonus requires 315 skill — there is no "free" elemental missile weapon at T6.

**Bows & Crossbows**

| Bonus | Wield Required | Chance |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| +1 to +4 | 315 (base) | 50% |
| +5 to +8 | 335 | 35% |
| **+9 to +12** | **360** | **15%** (the +12 alone is 2%) |

**Atlatls** — a shorter ladder, capping at +10 rather than +12:

| Bonus | Wield Required | Chance |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| +1 to +3 | 315 (base) | 50% |
| +4 to +6 | 335 | 35% |
| **+7 to +10** | **360** | **15%** (the +10 alone is 2%) |

### Non-Elemental (T1–T5 only)

> Within each family, the plain low-end variant (Shortbow, Light Crossbow, plain Atlatl) rolls from a weaker table than the standard variant (Longbow, Heavy Crossbow, Royal Atlatl).

| Weapon | T5 Damage Modifier | Wield Required |
|--------|--------------------|----------------|
| Heavy Crossbow | 2.47–2.55 | 290 |
| Royal Atlatl | 2.43–2.50 | 290 |
| Longbow / Light Crossbow / plain Atlatl | 2.23–2.30 | 290 |
| Shortbow | 1.70–2.10 | 270–290 |

*Lower tiers scale down from these: T1 has no wield requirement, T2–T3 gate at 250, T3–T4 at 270, T4+ at 290.*

### Missile Weapon Defense

| Stat | T6 Range | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Melee Defense Modifier | 1.08–1.15 | Set absolutely, not added — max **1.15** |
| Missile Defense Modifier | — | Never rolls (see below) |
| Magic Defense Modifier | — | Never rolls (see below) |

> ⚠️ **Known quirk:** missile weapons and wands are hardcoded to load the default (EoR) `weapon_defense` mutation script rather than the Infiltration one, and the default script has no Missile Defense or Magic Defense mutations in it. The practical result is that **only melee weapons ever roll those two modifiers on ClassicPvP.** The Melee Defense cap of 1.15 is the same either way.

> **⭐ Best-in-slot missile weapon:** a T6 elemental **crossbow** with Damage Modifier 2.55 and Elemental Damage Bonus +12 at wield 360 — the highest damage multiplier of any missile weapon in the game. The equivalent best bow is 2.30 / +12 and the best atlatl is 2.50 / +10. Expect most T6 elemental drops to sit in the +1 to +8 range.
