# ⚔️ ClassicPvP — Weapon Damage Feedback

> Tell us what you think end game weapon damage **should** be. Every number in this doc is
> **ideal end-game damage against a reasonably geared target** — a fully buffed,
> well-equipped opponent.

**Contents:** [Sword](#sword) · [Axe](#axe) · [Mace](#mace) · [Spear](#spear) · [Dagger](#dagger) · [Staff](#staff) · [Unarmed](#unarmed) · [Two Handed](#two-handed) · [Bow](#bow) · [Crossbow](#crossbow) · [Thrown](#thrown)


**Values are a min-max range for a single hit**, written like `Body: 30-49`.
Leave anything you have no opinion on blank.

| Term | Means |
|------|-------|
| **Body** | upper leg, lower leg, abdomen, chest, upper arm, lower arm |
| **Ext** | head, hands, foot |
| **Speed / Power** | attack stance the hit was made in |
| **Non-Crit / Crit** | normal hits vs critical hits |
| **Open World / Arena** | where the fight is happening — leave Arena blank if you think it should match Open World |
